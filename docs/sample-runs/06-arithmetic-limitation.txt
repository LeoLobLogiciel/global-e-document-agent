=== Observation ===

When asked the arithmetic in isolation, the agent computed it correctly 
and proactively corrected its previous error (visible in conversation 
history). This suggests Sonnet 4.5's arithmetic accuracy degrades when 
the operation is part of a complex multi-task response, not when it's 
the sole focus.

Implication for production: critical arithmetic should be delegated to 
a deterministic tool. Documented as known limitation in README.
